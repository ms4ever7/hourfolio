Your isles

Each hobby is its own island in a live ocean. Tap an island to fly over to it, then log a session and watch it grow. Drag to turn the map. Rest brings on the night.

today
Glow fades toward rest. Islands never shrink.
Island size & buildMastery
Levels grow the island, add trees and upgrade its landmark. There are five stages per hobby, from a lighthouse up to a grand beacon. It never goes backwards.
Glow, sparkles, lightsMomentum
Recent sessions light the ring in the water, the sparkles and the windows. With a quiet stretch it dims and a sleepy cloud appears, and one session wakes it.
Night fallsRest
Logging rest turns the whole world to evening and calms the sea. It's an activity you record, not a gap.
